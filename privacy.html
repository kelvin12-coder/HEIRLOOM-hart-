Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Heirloom is a free online game. This page explains in plain words what we keep, why, and how we protect it.

What we collect

  • Anonymous player ID: a random code made on your device so we can count players. It contains no personal information.
  • Account (optional): if you create an online save we store your username, a scrambled (hashed) password and your game save (money, cars, house, settings). We never store your password in readable form. We do not ask for your email or phone number.
  • Gameplay data: counts of players online, which places are visited, and cheating checks (for example impossible money jumps).
  • Feedback you send: the message you type, and optionally your username. Please do not type private details.
  • Photos you send: only to the one player you pick, kept 7 days, then deleted. Receivers can report, block or delete them.
  • Payments: if you buy coins, payment is handled by Paystack. We never see or store your card details. We keep the payment reference, the pack bought and the amount, so we can credit your game once.

Live players

While you play, your display name, position, outfit and car model are shared with nearby players through Supabase Realtime. This is not stored; it disappears when you leave.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The game uses your browser's local storage (similar to cookies) to keep your save, login token, settings, and your cookie choice. These are needed for the game to work. Clearing site data removes them. See the table below.

Third parties

  • Netlify (hosting and server functions)
  • Supabase (live player positions)
  • Paystack (payments)
  • Google Fonts (fonts; may receive your IP address)
  • Spotify, YouTube, Audiomack or SoundCloud only if you press play on music in the game

How we protect your data

  • Everything is sent over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are hashed with a slow, salted method (scrypt); accounts lock after repeated wrong passwords.
  • Secret keys (payments, admin) are kept only on the server in private settings, never inside the game page.
  • The server decides prices and checks payments directly with Paystack; the game page cannot set what you pay or what you receive.
  • Saves are checked for impossible values to stop cheating. Cheaters can be suspended.
  • Requests are rate limited and the page blocks outside scripts (Content Security Policy).

Children

Heirloom is for players aged 13 and over (16+ in some countries). Do not share personal details in chat. Parents with concerns can contact us below.

Your choices

  • Delete local data: Menu → Reset, or clear this site's data in your browser.
  • Delete your online account and save: contact us and we will remove it.
